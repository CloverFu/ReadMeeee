🔬 Research Focus研究方向

I develop process-based crop models, digital twins, and AI-driven decision-support systems for controlled-environment agriculture (CEA). My work integrates mechanistic modeling with machine learning and techno-economic analysis to enable autonomous, optimal, and sustainable plant factory operations.

我致力于为受控环境农业（CEA）开发基于过程的作物模型、数字孪生和AI驱动的决策支持系统。我的工作将机理建模与机器学习、工程经济分析相结合，实现植物工厂的自主化、最优化和可持续运营。

2026/08 – 2026/10

Huawei · Multi-Agent / LLM Engineer Intern Intern

  • Built a LangGraph-based multi-agent system using an orchestrator-worker pattern for case-based extraction of atomic, reusable capabilities to build standard SOP.基于LangGraph构建了orchestrator-worker模式的多智能体系统，通过案例推理提取原子化可复用能力，构建标准作业流程（SOP）。
2026/02 – Present

StrawberryFSPM: Functional-Structural Digital Twin Research

  • Developed an end-to-end 3D reconstruction workflow that recovers canopy structure and temporal traits from mobile video streams using 2D Gaussian Splatting.开发了端到端3D重建工作流，利用2D高斯泼溅（2D Gaussian Splatting）从移动视频流中恢复冠层结构和时间序列特征。
  • Coupled canopy geometry, ray tracing, and StrawberryGRO into a plant-specific digital twin for simulating light interception and growth response.将冠层几何、光线追踪与StrawberryGRO模型耦合，构建植物级数字孪生，用于模拟光截获与生长响应。
2025/03 – 2026/04

StrawberryGRO-E: Techno-Economic Management Framework Research

  • Proposed Simultaneous Active Inflorescences (SAI) as a reproductive-load index to explain yield heterogeneity across 50 plants, 475 inflorescences, and 1,130 fruits.提出"同时活性花序数"（SAI）作为生殖负载指数，解释了50株植物、475个花序、1,130个果实的产量异质性。
  • Developed a 365-day techno-economic decision-support framework that translates management strategies into yield, revenue, operating cost, and profit margin, enabling scenario-level comparison and quantitative evaluation of management alternatives.开发了365天技术经济决策支持框架，将管理策略转化为产量、收入、运营成本和利润率，实现场景级方案比较与定量评估。
2024/04 – 2025/02

Autonomous Greenhouse Challenge 2024 — Champion Champion

  • Led crop modeling, climate-control interaction, and decision-making within a "perception–decision–control–economics" pipeline, ensuring smooth cross-module data integration.在"感知-决策-控制-经济"管线中负责作物建模、气候控制交互与决策模块，确保跨模块数据流畅集成。
  • Modeled the interactions among climate setpoints, planting density, crop growth, fruit quality, energy cost, and market return to drive AI-based control decisions — achieving remote "zero-human-intervention" closed-loop operation over the 3-month competition.建模气候设定值、种植密度、作物生长、果实品质、能耗成本与市场回报之间的交互关系，驱动AI控制决策——在3个月赛期内实现远程"零人工干预"闭环运营。
  • Won the championship with profits 2× the runner-up; the first Chinese team to claim this title, covered by Zhejiang University official WeChat and mainstream domestic/international media (100k+ reads).以2倍于亚军的利润赢得冠军；首支获此荣誉的中国代表队，获浙江大学官方微信及国内外主流媒体报道（阅读量10万+）。
2022/12 – 2025/08

StrawberryGRO: Process-Based Growth and Harvest Modeling Research

  • Developed StrawberryGRO, the first process-based strawberry growth model for plant-factory, covering phenology, photosynthesis, assimilate partitioning, and periodic harvesting modules.开发了首个面向植物工厂的基于过程的草莓生长模型StrawberryGRO，涵盖物候期、光合作用、同化物分配和周期性采收模块。
  • Calibrated the model against long-cycle and light–temperature interaction datasets to simulate growth, cultivar response, and periodic harvesting.利用长周期和光温交互数据集标定模型，模拟生长、品种响应与周期性采收。
2021/12 – 2022/12

Multimodal Deep Learning for Lettuce Fresh-Weight Monitoring Research

  • Developed a multi-modal fusion deep-learning framework for automatic, non-destructive lettuce fresh-weight monitoring in plant factories.开发了多模态融合深度学习框架，用于植物工厂中生菜鲜重的自动无损监测。
